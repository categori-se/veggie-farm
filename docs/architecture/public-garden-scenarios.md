# Public garden planting scenarios

The demo plantings are proposed designs, not accession records or confirmed reconstructions. Dates are seasonal display windows for the current planning year, not perennial lifespans. Existing saved plantings remain intact; a one-time enrichment fills empty beds in public demo workspaces. Later intentional deletions remain deleted. Private gardens are excluded.

## Sources consulted on 2026-09-26

- [BBG visitor guide](https://www.berkshirebotanical.org/sites/default/files/7_14_20_kiosk%20map.pdf) identifies edible, children’s vegetable, herb and flower garden themes. [Herb Garden](https://www.berkshirebotanical.org/herb-garden) supplies historical and planting context. Vegetable and herb combinations are our design proposals, not observed cultivar identifications.
- [The Mount gardens](https://edithwharton.org/the-gardens/) describes kitchen-garden history, formal flower beds and phlox, dianthus, lilies and other flowers. Its photo gallery informs the use of mixed borders; individual planting positions are inferred.
- [Naumkeag](https://thetrustees.org/place/naumkeag/) documents seasonal flower displays. [TCLF photographs](https://www.tclf.org/naumkeag) illustrate planted terraces and structural vegetation. Proposed flower palettes approximate that experience; they do not reproduce a particular year’s inventory.
- [Ashintully](https://thetrustees.org/place/ashintully-gardens/) provides landscape and garden context. The demo’s seasonal borders are an imaginative design study, not verified species records.

No third-party photographs or models were copied into this source release. Palette choices use the existing planner plant catalog. Cool-season greens, summer tomato/herb combinations and late-season greens have different planning windows. Flower borders mix available catalog flowers. Exact dates, density and species should be reviewed when adapting a demo to a real garden.

## Current physical limits

Walking and shade calculations still assume flat ground. Identified DEM/LiDAR products have not yet been processed into scene terrain or measured canopy models. Decorative lighting is fixed in world coordinates; date/time sunlight previews are a separate calculated mode. Bed picking prioritizes actual bed intersections through flat context overlays, while solid buildings retain occlusion.

# Soil reports in a garden's history

Saved Notebook soil reports can be linked explicitly to a stable garden bed ID. The gardener chooses the report, chooses the bed, and confirms the sample represents that bed. The original Notebook report remains unchanged. The linked snapshot retains its original area label, Notebook revision, laboratory, sample date, pH, organic matter, nutrient text and report text. Unlinking removes only the garden association. Duplicate links within the same garden are rejected.

My Garden shows linked soil records through the selected year and applies its existing bed filter. Groups use exact bed identity and laboratory text; different beds/laboratories do not become one series. A chronological pH sequence requires multiple dated numeric readings, a recorded laboratory, and distinct sample dates. Unknown pH remains unknown, zero remains a recorded value, and future reports are excluded. Same-day duplicates and missing laboratories retain their report details without an ordered pH sequence.

The recorded fields do not establish matching sample depth or laboratory method. Source details remain available for the gardener to assess comparability. No amendment rate, crop suitability or cause of a change is inferred. Existing Notebook charts remain available; reports are not automatically assigned using similar names.

Account changes use the existing journal revision/owner protection. The linked records live in garden properties and survive account restoration into Studio and JSON backups. There is no new storage service or background synchronization. Browser acceptance uses synthetic account records; real authenticated cross-device acceptance is still outstanding.

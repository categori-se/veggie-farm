# Today → dated planning choice

Today’s crop comparison offers Plan this crop. Its URL fragment carries the public crop-guide identity and the entered planning date, with no garden contents or credentials. Generic crop choices have no invented cultivar or numeric dimensions. Find Plants remains the path to a specific catalog variety.

Plan opens the existing planning tray. Users explicitly choose a destination garden/bed and review spacing, width and height. The incoming date initializes the year and optional planned-date field; a supplied date must be valid and in the selected year. Different dates create separate tray choices for succession. Legacy undated tray choices stay supported.

Arrange in this bed selects the plant and previews the saved date. Explicitly adding plants carries that proposed date into the existing planned planting field, not an observed event. Saving a tray choice does not place a plant. Account backups retain both the tray date and planting plan; existing rollback applies. Consuming the plant fragment preserves unrelated fragment parameters.

The selected account garden/bed handoff is now implemented in source, pending hosted and real-account acceptance. Cancelling keeps the crop choice, with an explicit option to choose another garden; a missing bed or signed-out session cannot silently use a local destination.

# Review observations before repeating a season

My Garden's season review compares the selected year with the preceding year, grouped by exact catalog plant identity and bed identity. It uses only dated, planting-linked observations at or before today. Foreign planting IDs, changed-crop events, invalid dates and future observations are excluded.

Harvest weight is converted to kilograms; counts and records without usable quantities remain separate. First and last recorded harvest dates are shown. Missing history is unknown, not zero production. Differences in plant counts, conditions and recording habits prevent these totals from establishing yield or performance.

Bolting, pest sightings, possible disease, heat damage and frost damage recorded in both years are surfaced for review, without inferring a cause or confirming a diagnosis. Expanding each comparison shows the underlying dated notes.

Timing comparisons require an explicit previous-season planting link, the same plant and bed, one matching sow/transplant event in each year, and a first harvest after that event. They compare recorded intervals, not proven maturity. Multiple entry events leave that comparison unknown.

The review does not mutate the garden. Its next-year action opens the existing review/create workflow for the selected source year. No background requests, additional AWS services, inferred observations or external advice are involved. Four comparisons appear initially, with more available explicitly.

Tests cover mixed units, absent data, invalid/foreign/future events, ambiguous timing and crop/bed separation. Real Studio acceptance uses synthetic two-year records to verify totals, recurring issues, underlying notes, mobile layout and the next-year handoff.

Still incomplete: soil measurement trends, saved gardener lessons, condition-adjusted comparisons, photo history and authenticated cross-device acceptance. This is one part of the annual learning loop, not a complete recommendation engine.

# Decision-led gardening journey

Owner direction, September 27, 2026; supplements the full [gardening journey goal](garden-journey-goal.md) and [plant workspace](plant-planning-workspace.md). This is requested product behavior, not a completion claim.

Today remains the primary question-oriented entry: current weather, frost context, planting now, preparing next spring, and relevant monthly tasks/articles. Studio is the spatial part of the same application, even across a subdomain. Articles and decision tools should lead into the gardener's own plan and observations.

Garden context should inform decisions: location and hardiness, dated soil readings and test guidance, light and obstruction estimates, plant spacing/competition, and growing season. Explain missing data and assumptions. The owner's conversational crop/spacing examples are not horticultural thresholds; recommendations need appropriate evidence, cultivar context and units.

Months and years should be explicit filters for plans, recorded observations and weather/climate comparisons. Keep historical observations separate from proposed plans, estimates and future forecasts.

Plant priorities are personal: choices made in Find Plants should be remembered with the user's account, surface throughout the application, and lead the Studio planting library. The full catalog remains available to add more. Do not confuse a comparison shortlist, plants present in a demo, plants already grown, or a garden-specific planning tray with explicit account-wide favorites.

Initial implementation: Studio offers the active garden's tray choices and existing plants before the full library; the 3D gallery puts the same choices first. This uses existing garden records and does not create new preference data. Remaining work includes explicit account-wide priorities, cross-domain continuity, year-specific priorities, garden-aware Today, contextual fit/recommendations and monthly guidance integration.

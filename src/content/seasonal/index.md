## Let your garden set the date

These notes describe a New England rhythm, not fixed appointments. Elevation, coast, frost pockets, soil, exposure, and the year itself can move every window.

Use the series with [Garden Today](/tools/today): enter your own frost dates and seedbed temperature, compare crop timing, and treat the articles as the explanatory layer behind the result.

<div class="field-notes">
  <p>Record the date beside each useful observation. “Peony spots” is a memory. “First spots on August 3 after a wet week; lower leaves first” is garden knowledge.</p>
</div>

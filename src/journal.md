# Garden journal

Record observations, photos and harvests in the garden they belong to.

Choose a saved garden below. Its records open in a focused journal workspace.

```js
import {accountGardenHome} from "./components/account-garden-home.js";
display(accountGardenHome({invalidation, initialView: "journal"}));
```
